About MENSERCA

Industrial contracting with regional reach and deep technical execution.

Founded in 1992, MENSERCA is a leading contractor with 34 years of uninterrupted experience in engineering, maintenance, and construction services for the oil and industrial sectors.

We deliver comprehensive solutions supported by a highly skilled workforce, advanced technology, and top-tier proprietary equipment.

With strong operational foundations in Zulia and Anzoátegui, and a key presence in El Tigre, we are strategically positioned to serve critical energy and industrial projects across the region.
